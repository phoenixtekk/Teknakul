## Start here

### What is Teknakul?
A creator-owned video platform for technical people — MSPs, IT pros, entrepreneurs, and creators.
Watch and publish code-alongs, homelab tours, Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace / Intune / security
walkthroughs, and build-in-public. The mission: help you run complex tech confidently, using AI as a
force multiplier — by *watching someone do it*, then doing it yourself.

### Watching videos
Browse from the homepage or **Browse videos**. No account needed to watch public videos. Each video has
adaptive quality (360p–1080p) so it plays on slow connections and mobile.

### Creating an account & signing in 📋
Three ways (once enabled): **email/password**, **Microsoft**, or **Google**. One account, one profile.
*(Auth is being wired up — see the roadmap.)*

### Your handle and "federated" 📋
Your channel address looks like `@yourchannel@teknakul.com`. Teknakul is part of the **Fediverse** — an
open network — so people on Mastodon, other PeerTube sites, etc. can follow your channel without an
account here. Your audience is yours; it isn't locked to one platform.

## For creators

- **Channels** — you can have multiple channels under one account (e.g., one per topic). 📋
- **Uploading a video** — drag-and-drop; large files upload in resumable chunks. Choose a category
  (Intune, M365, Google Workspace, Security, Linux, Homelab, Build-in-public), add a description,
  thumbnail, and visibility (public / unlisted / private). 📋
- **After upload** — Teknakul transcodes your video to multiple qualities automatically (this can take
  a while on busy days). 📋
- **Playlists** — group videos into series / courses. 📋
- **Live streaming** 📋 — Twitch-style live (Phase 2). You'll get an ingest URL + stream key to use in
  OBS or similar.

## Runbook Mode — the AI features ✅
Teknakul reads your video so viewers can learn faster. It happens automatically after you upload —
nothing to configure.

- **Auto captions/transcript** — every video with speech is transcribed. Viewers can turn captions on,
  and the transcript makes the video searchable.
- **AI chapters** — the video timeline is split into labelled sections (e.g. *0:14 Create the policy*).
  Viewers click a chapter to jump straight to it. You can edit or add chapters yourself in
  **Edit video → Chapters** if you want to fine-tune them.
- **AI summary** — a short plain-English summary of what the video teaches is added to the description
  (under an *"✨ AI Summary"* heading). Edit or remove it any time by editing the description.
- **Timing** — chapters and the summary appear a few minutes after upload, once transcoding and
  transcription finish. If you don't see them yet, check back shortly.

*Coming next:* ask a question about a video and jump to the answer, a copy-ready commands panel, and
searching across every video by what was said.
