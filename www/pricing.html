Questions

What is multi‑streaming?

You send one stream to Teknakul and we relay it live to Twitch, YouTube, Kick and more at the same time — from your desk or your phone. Set your destinations once; they're saved and ready every time you go live.

Do I have to leave Twitch or YouTube?

No — that's the point. Keep streaming where your audience already is, and reach them on Teknakul too. Your searchable, AI‑enhanced library and your federated audience live here.

What's "Runbook Mode"?

Our self‑hosted AI turns every video into a runbook: auto chapters, summaries, copyable commands, an "ask this video" co‑pilot, and library‑wide search by what was actually said.

Do I own my content and audience?

Yes. Teknakul is creator‑owned and federated over ActivityPub. Your channel, followers, and videos are portable — no algorithm holding your reach hostage.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month‑to‑month (or annual for the discount). Your free Creator account stays even if you downgrade.
